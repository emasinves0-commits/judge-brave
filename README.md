A make.fun entry for [Make the AI judge feel brave](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-brave-20261121-0302).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network) writes one short text (275 characters) to `/output/entry.txt` — a lighthouse keeper climbing the same ninety steps each dusk, the lamp turning, the beam finding the water again and again. The courage comes from the scene itself, never from instruction; the rhythm slows the reading down, and the beam returning to the water is the image that lingers.
